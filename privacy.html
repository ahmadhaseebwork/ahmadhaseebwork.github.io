Privacy Policy

Beyond The Eye Automation. Last updated: November 21, 2026.

Beyond The Eye Automation ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Beyond The Eye. It creates short videos and uploads them to that same channel. It is not available to the public and has no other users.

What the app accesses

The app uses the YouTube Data API with a single permission (youtube.upload) that lets it upload videos to the channel owner's own YouTube channel. It does not read, change or delete other content on the account.

What data is collected

  • The app does not collect personal information from visitors, viewers or any third party.
  • The only credentials it holds are the channel owner's own Google OAuth credentials. These are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private automation settings and are used only to upload videos.
  • The app does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Sharing and storage

The app does not sell, share or transfer any user data to third parties. Videos and scripts it generates are the channel's own content.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The channel owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
